  <h1>👑 Royal Dum Biryani</h1>
  <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1563379091339-03b21ab4a4f8?q=80&w=400&auto=format&fit=crop" alt="Delicious Biryani">

  <ul class="steps-list">
    <li><b>Soak the Rice:</b> Wash 500g of long-grain Basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 30 minutes.</li>
    <li><b>Marination:</b> Mix 500g of chicken with 1 cup of yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, biryani masala, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt. Let it rest for at least 1 hour.</li>
    <li><b>Boil Water:</b> Boil water in a large pot. Add whole spices (cloves, cardamom, bay leaf, cinnamon) and salt.</li>
    <li><b>Cook the Rice:</b> Add the soaked rice to the boiling water. Cook it only until it is 70% done, then drain the water completely.</li>
    <li><b>Heat Ghee:</b> Heat 3-4 tablespoons of pure Ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot.</li>
    <li><b>Cook the Chicken:</b> Add the marinated chicken to the hot ghee and cook until the oil starts separating from the masala.</li>
    <li><b>Layering:</b> Spread the 70% cooked rice evenly over the cooked chicken to form a flat layer.</li>
    <li><b>Garnish:</b> Top the rice with crispy fried onions (birista) and freshly chopped mint and coriander leaves.</li>
    <li><b>Saffron Milk:</b> Sprinkle a tablespoon of warm milk infused with saffron over the rice for a rich royal color and aroma.</li>
    <li><b>Seal for Dum:</b> Seal the pot tightly with aluminum foil or wheat dough so that no steam can escape.</li>
    <li><b>Slow Cook:</b> Place the pot over a hot tawa (griddle) and let it cook on very low heat for 15-20 minutes on 'Dum'.</li>
    <li><b>Serve:</b> Turn off the heat and wait 10 minutes before opening the pot. Serve the hot, aromatic biryani with raita!</li>
  </ul>
